Pro Perspectives

These aren’t just testimonials — they’re moments of mentorship. Each conversation added something valuable to our journey, making us feel seen, guided, and ready to level up.

At Blanka Botz , Since 2011 has grown from a small-town robotics club into one of India’s most celebrated combat robotics teams. With championship robots like Taanaji, Dara, and Dhanaji, we have secured victories at Techfest IIT Bombay, set knockout records, and made history as FMB World Champions in China. Our journey has spanned over 500 events worldwide, including Russia’s BITVA Robotov and the Games of the Future 2024, cementing our place on the global map of combat robotics.

For us, victories are only part of the story. Through mentorship, workshops, and Technobotix Pvt Ltd, we’ve worked to strengthen the Indian robotics community, making resources and guidance accessible to teams across the country.

Among those teams, the Texcelerators stand out as passionate and determined builders. Working alongside them has shown us a spirit of creativity and commitment that reminds us of why we started our own journey. We believe Texcelerators have the potential to become one of India’s most promising combat teams, and we are proud to call them our close allies in this ever-growing community.
Shahabaj Gawali
Pilot, Team Blanka Botz
At Prixx Robotics, we have evolved from passionate competitors into one of India’s recognized combat robotics startups. With victories at Techfest IIT Mumbai, VJTI, NIT Warangal, and international stages in Shanghai, Las Vegas, and RoboGames USA, our journey reflects a belief that innovation is not just about machines—it’s about mindset. Beyond building combat-ready bots, we’ve also supported teams across India and abroad with machining, custom electronics, and training, strengthening the robotics community as a whole.

What sets Prixx apart is the balance of engineering excellence and community building. From launching Prixx Works to provide machining and 3D printing solutions, to delivering international orders and mentoring Indian robotics teams, we’ve always believed in growing together. Our vision has been clear: not just to win battles, but to empower builders, create opportunities, and keep India’s combat robotics spirit alive on the global stage.

It is in this spirit that we value our bond with the Texcelerators. Their determination, creativity, and discipline remind us of our own beginnings, and we are proud to support them in their Robowars journey. For us, they are not just another team—we see in them the next generation of roboteers who will carry India’s legacy forward
Sushant Barde
Founder, Prixx Robotics
