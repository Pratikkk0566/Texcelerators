Workshop Procedure

A step-by-step guide to our workshop process

1. Registration

Fill out our registration form with your details and preferred workshop tracks.

2. Confirmation

Receive confirmation email with workshop schedule and preparation materials.

3. Hands-on Learning

Participate in interactive sessions with practical projects and expert guidance.

4. Certification

Complete projects and receive certificates of completion and achievement.
