OUR COMBAT BOT

The Legend of Raudra

Competition Ready
Blueprint
Fabrication

Raudra — meaning "fierce" or "wrathful" in Sanskrit — is the combat robot that carries the heart and soul of Texcelrators into the arena. Every weld, every bolt, every circuit reflects countless nights of dedication.

Built to dominate the 15kg weight class, Raudra is engineered for one purpose: to win. From its hardened chassis to its devastating weapon system, every design decision was made with battle in mind.

From humble sketches on paper to a competition-hardened machine — Raudra's journey is the story of Texcelrators' growth, grit, and glory.

COMPETITION HISTORY

Raudra's Battle Journey

We're just getting started — but every arena shapes us.

Debut Victory 🏆
February 2026 SGGSIE&T Nanded, Maharashtra🏆

SGGSIE&T Nanded – Armageddon 2026

Most teams enter the arena hoping to survive. We entered to take over. At Armageddon 2026, SGGSIE&T Nanded, our 16 kg bot made its competitive debut and left no room for doubt. Opponents were outdriven, outmatched, and outgunned as we carved through the bracket with relentless aggression. What was supposed to be our introduction became a complete demolition of the competition. We didn't just win our first Robowars event—we announced our arrival.

April 2026 Mahindra University, Hyderabad

Mahindra University Hyderabad – Aeon 2026

Our second outing was even more explosive. We fought aggressively through the bracket, including a devastating one-shot knockout that left an opponent in flames. After reaching the semifinals, a brutal battle left our bot heavily damaged. Despite a frantic repair effort, time ran out before we could return to the arena, ending our run with a hard-fought disqualification.
